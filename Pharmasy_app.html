MediConnect - Appointment App

Project Description

A mobile healthcare appointment and medical shopping app that lets users find doctors, book online/offline consultations, and manage upcoming or past appointments seamlessly. It also integrates an in-app medical store for purchasing healthcare products, offering a complete digital health solution in one platform.
